12. See It Evolve: Geomungo Factory

Contemporary musicians continue to experiment with the geomungo, changing its design, adding strings, and exploring new ways of producing sound.

In this performance by Geomungo Factory, some instruments and techniques may look familiar, while others are noticeably different. You may see geomungos with additional strings, traditional suldae playing, and even one played with a bow.

You may also spot the geomungo’s often-confused cousin, the gayageum, another traditional Korean stringed instrument with a similar appearance.
