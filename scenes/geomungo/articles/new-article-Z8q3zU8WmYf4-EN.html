13. From Tradition to Global Pop

The sound of the geomungo has also found its way into contemporary popular music. In 2022, BLACKPINK, one of the world’s most popular K-pop groups, featured the instrument in the opening of their hit song “Pink Venom.”

From an instrument with roots in ancient Korea to a sound heard by audiences around the world, the geomungo continues to evolve while remaining connected to its long musical tradition.
