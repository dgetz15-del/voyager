11. See The Tradition: Geomungo Sanjo

Sanjo is a traditional Korean instrumental genre built around a solo performer accompanied by a janggu drum. The music moves through changing rhythms and gives the performer room for expressive variations in pitch, tone, and dynamics.

As you watch, look for techniques you’ve already explored: the right hand striking the strings with the suldae and the left hand pressing and manipulating the strings to shape each note.
