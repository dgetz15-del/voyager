2. Ancient Roots

The geomungo traces its history to Goguryeo, one of the ancient Three Kingdoms of Korea.

According to a centuries-old tradition, the musician Wang San-ak created the instrument by adapting an earlier Chinese zither. While the exact origins of the geomungo are difficult to reconstruct, depictions of the instrument appear in Goguryeo tomb murals dating back more than 1,500 years.

Image: Jeong Gi-hwan, Muyongchong Tangeumdo I (reproduction), National Museum of Korea.

These surviving images provide a remarkable glimpse of the geomungo's place in ancient Korean musical culture—a tradition that has continued to evolve for centuries.
